Method

Two one-factor state-space models estimated by maximum likelihood (Kalman filter). Model 2, shown above, splits LFS unemployment at January 2020: both halves load on the common factor with coefficient one, the pre-2020 half with near-zero measurement error, so the factor is scaled in unemployment-rate units and post-2020 LFS unemployment is treated as one noisy indicator among several. All other indicators get random-walk drifts, so only their co-movement with the factor is used. Series in the model: LFS unemployment rate (16+, SA), Claimant count rate (SA), Average actual weekly hours, full-time (SA), Vacancies per payrolled employee, Private regular pay, 3m/3m annualised growth, RF alternative employment rate, 16-64, Underemployment rate (NSA), BoE Agents' recruitment difficulties score, DMP ease of hiring, net balance, Indeed job postings index. BCC recruitment difficulty is shown in the table but not in the model, as in the original. Rolling 3-month LFS series are dated at the middle month of the window (ONS convention).
